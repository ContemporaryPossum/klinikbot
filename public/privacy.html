Privacy Policy

KlinikBot · Last updated 29 October 2026 · Versi Bahasa Melayu di bawah

KlinikBot is a WhatsApp appointment assistant used by clinics in Malaysia to book, remind, reschedule and cancel appointments, and to answer simple questions about the clinic. This policy explains what information the service handles when you message a clinic that uses KlinikBot.

1. Information we process

  • WhatsApp phone number and the profile name WhatsApp shares with businesses.
  • Messages you send to the clinic's WhatsApp number and the replies sent to you.
  • Booking details you give us: your name, the service requested, and the appointment date and time.
  • Technical logs needed to run the service (timestamps, delivery status).

We do not ask for, and you should not send, identity card numbers, payment card numbers or detailed medical information through the assistant. If a clinic asks you to confirm a deposit, it is done through the clinic's own payment channel.

2. Why we use it

  • To book, confirm, reschedule or cancel your appointment with the clinic.
  • To send appointment reminders (typically the day before and shortly before the appointment).
  • To answer your questions about opening hours, location, services and prices.
  • To hand your conversation over to clinic staff when you ask for a person.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Who receives it

  • The clinic you are messaging — it is the clinic's appointment book; clinic staff see your bookings and messages.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) — delivers the messages. Meta's handling is governed by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the service and stores the appointment data.
  • Anthropic — when a clinic enables AI answers, the text of your question (without your phone number) may be processed by the Claude API to compose a reply about the clinic's services. It is not used to train models.

4. How long we keep it

Conversation history is kept only as long as needed to manage your appointment and is routinely cleared. Booking records are kept for the clinic's record-keeping period and deleted or anonymised when the clinic stops using the service.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Reply "stop" or "berhenti" at any time to stop receiving reminders.
  • You may ask the clinic to view, correct or delete the information held about you, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia).
  • You can block or report the number in WhatsApp at any time.

6. Security

Messages travel over WhatsApp's encrypted transport. Data at rest is stored in Cloudflare's infrastructure with access limited to the service and the clinic's dashboard. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep sensitive information out of chat.

7. Children

The service is intended for adults booking for themselves or their dependants. Bookings for children are made by a parent or guardian.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions about your data can be sent to the clinic you are messaging, or to the KlinikBot operator through that clinic. Clinic-specific contact details appear in the clinic's WhatsApp business profile.

Dasar Privasi (Bahasa Melayu)

KlinikBot ialah pembantu temujanji WhatsApp yang digunakan oleh klinik di Malaysia untuk menempah, mengingatkan, menukar dan membatalkan temujanji serta menjawab soalan ringkas tentang klinik.

Maklumat yang diproses

Nombor WhatsApp dan nama profil anda, mesej yang anda hantar kepada klinik dan balasan yang dihantar, serta butiran tempahan (nama, perkhidmatan, tarikh dan masa). Jangan hantar nombor kad pengenalan, nombor kad pembayaran atau maklumat perubatan terperinci melalui pembantu ini.

Tujuan

Untuk menguruskan temujanji anda, menghantar peringatan, menjawab soalan tentang waktu operasi, lokasi, perkhidmatan dan harga, dan menyerahkan perbualan kepada staf klinik apabila diminta. Maklumat anda tidak dijual dan tidak digunakan untuk pengiklanan.

Penerima

Klinik yang anda hubungi, Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), Cloudflare (pengehosan dan storan), dan Anthropic (API Claude) apabila klinik mengaktifkan jawapan AI — teks soalan anda diproses tanpa nombor telefon anda.

Hak anda

Balas "berhenti" untuk menghentikan peringatan. Anda boleh meminta klinik untuk melihat, membetulkan atau memadam maklumat anda selaras dengan Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010. Sebarang pertanyaan boleh dihantar kepada klinik yang anda hubungi.
